Candidate Testing Protocols & Exam Day Readiness

Essential requirements, identification rules, and procedural checklists for physical test centers and online OnVUE proctoring.

1. Identification Requirements

You must bring two valid forms of ID:

  • Primary ID: Government-issued, original, unexpired with candidate's full legal name, recognizable photograph, and signature (Passport, Driver's License, National ID Card).
  • Secondary ID: Must contain candidate's printed name and matching signature (Credit card, Debit card, Employee ID).
  • Both names must match the exact spelling on your Pearson VUE / LPI account profile.

2. In-Person Test Center Protocol

Procedures at accredited Pearson VUE testing centers:

  • Arrive 30 minutes early before your scheduled appointment time for identity verification and biometric scanning.
  • All personal belongings (phones, smartwatches, bags, wallets, keys, jackets) must be stored in secure lockers provided by the facility.
  • You will be provided with an erasable notepad whiteboard and marker for rough calculations.

3. OnVUE Online Remote Proctoring

If taking the exam from your home or private office:

  • Run the official System Test prior to exam day to ensure webcam, microphone, and internet speed (>3 Mbps) meet standards.
  • Your testing desk must be completely clear of all papers, books, extra monitors, writing utensils, and phones.
  • The test room must be completely private with no other individuals entering or speaking during the 90-minute testing session.

4. Retake Policy & Official Scoring

Rules governing scores, certifications, and retakes:

  • LPIC-1 passing mark is 500 out of 800 (scaled score based on question weighting).
  • If you do not pass on your first attempt, you must wait 7 days before retaking.
  • For any subsequent attempts, a 30-day waiting period applies.
  • Once passed, LPIC-1 credentials remain active and verifiable for 5 years.
